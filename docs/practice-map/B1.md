# B1 Practice Map

Status: **first full map drafted**

This is a level library map, not a required lesson sequence.

`Core` means **Great place to start**, not prerequisite.

## Design boundary

B1 Practice should feel like reasonably independent communication in familiar life situations.

Typical B1 mission shape:

- one clear real-world goal with a **meaningful complication**
- roughly 5–9 meaningful learner moves, depending on branch
- connected answers rather than isolated phrases
- learner-owned follow-up questions
- brief reasons/explanations and short narration where relevant
- more than one plausible route to a valid ending
- partner remains realistic and cooperative/neutral rather than artificially difficult
- dynamic hints are optional support rather than expected scaffolding

B1 may include a complaint, misunderstanding, changed plan, unavailable option, or practical problem. It should **not** normally require management of several competing constraints, strategic negotiation, sustained disagreement, or nuanced register/tact; those become central at B2+.

## Core / Great place to start

These eight missions demonstrate the main B1 step beyond A2 across all seven worlds:

1. `B1-EV-01` — Explain a mix-up and fix the arrangement
2. `B1-PS-01` — Keep an unprepared familiar conversation going
3. `B1-FS-01` — Resolve a wrong order
4. `B1-TT-01` — Explain a travel problem and find the next step
5. `B1-TT-02` — Change a hotel booking and handle an alternative
6. `B1-WS-01` — Resolve a work or study misunderstanding
7. `B1-HS-01` — Explain a recurring service problem and arrange a solution
8. `B1-PL-01` — Re-plan an outing when something changes

Listening Preview is **selective/optional** at B1. Include it only when hearing one controlled exemplar materially helps the learner understand the interaction shape.

# Mission library

## Everyday Life — 4 missions

### B1-EV-01 — Explain a mix-up and fix the arrangement ★ Core

**Goal:** explain how a routine arrangement/message was misunderstood, identify the actual intended detail, and agree the corrected plan.

**Communication problem:** `explain misunderstanding + clarify + repair arrangement`

**Likely shape:** discover mismatch -> explain what learner understood -> compare with intended detail -> ask/check one point -> agree corrected plan -> confirm.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 clarifies one unclear detail. B1 explains an actual misunderstanding after it has affected the plan and helps repair the consequence.

**Branch potential:** either learner misunderstood the time/place, or partner did; both can reach a corrected arrangement.

**Listening Preview:** optional/recommended for first-time B1 orientation.

---

### B1-EV-02 — Tell what happened after a small everyday problem

**Goal:** explain a short sequence of events around a familiar problem and describe the result/reaction clearly enough for another person to understand.

**Communication problem:** `short narration + explanation + reaction`

**Possible contexts:** missed delivery, lost keys found later, phone stopped working, wrong bus, forgotten item, appointment mix-up.

**Likely shape:** state problem -> explain what happened before/after -> answer follow-up -> say result/feeling -> discuss one next step or close.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 gives a few past facts. B1 connects them into a short account with cause/result or reaction.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

---

### B1-EV-03 — Solve a routine problem on the phone

**Goal:** make a phone call about a familiar problem, explain enough context, answer follow-up questions, and agree one next action.

**Communication problem:** `phone problem explanation + information exchange + solution`

**Possible contexts:** delivery time, booking issue, missing confirmation, service appointment, item collection.

**Likely shape:** identify purpose -> explain problem/context -> answer detail questions -> hear proposed next step -> accept or ask one alternative -> confirm.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 handles predictable phone details. B1 must carry a small problem through to a solution without visual support.

**Listening Preview:** recommended when authored because phone-only turn-taking is useful input.

---

### B1-EV-04 — Ask for advice about a practical problem

**Goal:** explain a familiar practical problem, ask for advice, react to one or two suggestions, and choose a reasonable next step.

**Communication problem:** `explain situation + ask advice + evaluate solution`

**Likely shape:** explain problem -> partner asks detail -> give detail -> receive suggestion -> react/reason -> choose/ask follow-up -> close.

**B1 boundary:** advice topic must remain ordinary and low-stakes. Avoid medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical decisions as mission content.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

## People & Social — 3 missions

### B1-PS-01 — Keep an unprepared familiar conversation going ★ Core

**Goal:** enter a familiar conversation without a memorised script, exchange opinions/details, ask relevant follow-ups, and keep it moving naturally for several turns.

**Communication problem:** `unprepared conversation + learner initiative + follow-up`

**Possible topics:** work/study life, hobbies, travel, neighbourhood, entertainment, recent plans.

**Likely shape:** natural opening -> partner offers topic -> learner responds with detail/opinion -> asks follow-up -> reacts to new information -> develops one subtopic -> close/transition.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 sustains a short guided exchange. B1 takes more responsibility for maintaining and developing the conversation.

**Authoring note:** mission still needs authored truth/topic beats; this is not Free Speak.

**Listening Preview:** usually unnecessary.

---

### B1-PS-02 — Tell a short story about an experience

**Goal:** tell a friend about a familiar experience with a clear sequence, one notable event, and feelings/reactions, then answer follow-ups.

**Communication problem:** `connected narration + reaction + follow-up`

**Possible experiences:** day trip, event, first day somewhere, funny mistake, activity, journey, celebration.

**Likely shape:** set context -> what happened -> notable moment -> result/feeling -> partner follow-up -> answer/reaction.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 reports recent activities. B1 organises a short story and makes the point of the experience clear.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

---

### B1-PS-03 — Clear up a social misunderstanding

**Goal:** notice that a friend/acquaintance misunderstood a familiar comment or plan, explain what was meant, respond to their perspective, and restore a clear shared understanding.

**Communication problem:** `interpersonal repair + explanation + reaction`

**Likely shape:** partner reveals misunderstanding -> learner clarifies intended meaning -> gives brief reason/context -> partner reacts/questions -> learner confirms/adjusts -> close.

**B1 boundary:** keep stakes mild. Sensitive conflict requiring tactful relationship management belongs at C1; sustained disagreement belongs at B2+.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

## Food & Shopping — 4 missions

### B1-FS-01 — Resolve a wrong order ★ Core

**Goal:** explain that the received order is different from what was requested, identify the mismatch, and reach a straightforward correction.

**Communication problem:** `complaint + explanation + solution branch`

**Likely shape:** receive wrong item -> explain expected vs received -> answer/check detail -> partner offers correction -> accept, or clarify once if correction is still wrong -> close.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 customises an order. B1 handles a meaningful service failure after the order has already happened.

**Branch potential:** direct replacement; unavailable original item leading to one simple alternative; partner asks learner to confirm original choice.

**Listening Preview:** recommended for an early B1 Core mission.

---

### B1-FS-02 — Make a simple complaint and request action

**Goal:** describe a clear problem with a familiar product/service, explain its impact briefly, and request a reasonable concrete action.

**Communication problem:** `complaint + supporting detail + request action`

**Possible outcomes:** replacement, refund under clear scenario truth, redo/correction, staff check, delivery update.

**Likely shape:** state complaint -> explain evidence/detail -> answer one question -> request action -> partner offers straightforward response -> confirm/close.

**B1 boundary:** no prolonged policy argument, leverage, compensation bargaining, or adversarial negotiation.

**Listening Preview:** optional/recommended.

---

### B1-FS-03 — Ask for a product recommendation for a specific need

**Goal:** explain what the item is needed for, give two or three relevant requirements, react to a recommendation, and decide whether it fits.

**Communication problem:** `explain needs + receive recommendation + evaluate`

**Likely shape:** state use case -> answer follow-up requirements -> receive recommendation -> ask one detail -> explain fit/non-fit -> choose or request one alternative -> close.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 compares two obvious products. B1 first explains a use case and lets the recommendation emerge from those needs.

**B1 boundary:** requirements remain familiar; strategic trade-off negotiation belongs at B2.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

---

### B1-FS-04 — Handle an unavailable item and find a workable alternative

**Goal:** explain what you need when the intended item is unavailable, evaluate one or two alternatives, and choose a workable substitute or decide not to buy.

**Communication problem:** `unavailable option + explain need + solution choice`

**Likely shape:** request item -> learn unavailable -> explain important feature/need -> partner proposes alternative -> ask/check detail -> accept/reject -> optional second path -> close.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 chooses from available options. B1 adapts after the expected option disappears.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

## Travel & Transport — 4 missions

### B1-TT-01 — Explain a travel problem and find the next step ★ Core

**Goal:** explain a familiar travel disruption, give relevant details, ask what options exist, and select a practical next step.

**Communication problem:** `travel problem + explanation + solution`

**Possible problems:** delay, missed connection, wrong platform information, cancelled local service, lost reservation confirmation.

**Likely shape:** report problem -> explain journey/details -> partner checks one fact -> offers option(s) -> learner asks follow-up -> chooses/clarifies -> confirms.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 compares normal transport options. B1 responds when the planned journey stops working.

**B1 boundary:** one main disruption and a small solution space. Complex multi-leg replanning under time/cost trade-offs belongs at B2.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### B1-TT-02 — Change a hotel booking and handle an alternative ★ Core

**Goal:** request a meaningful change to a hotel booking, explain the reason briefly, and accept or reject one offered alternative before reaching a workable arrangement.

**Communication problem:** `change booking + explanation + branch decision`

**Likely shape:** identify booking -> request change -> explain reason -> requested option unavailable/partly available -> partner offers alternative -> accept OR reject and ask one other possibility -> confirm.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 changes one detail from cooperative options. B1 handles the requested change not being available exactly as asked.

**Privacy rule:** booking identity/details are fictional or placeholders.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### B1-TT-03 — Report a hotel room problem and request a solution

**Goal:** describe a room/service problem with enough detail, answer a clarification, and agree a reasonable solution.

**Communication problem:** `service complaint + describe impact + solution`

**Possible problems:** noise, heating/air issue, missing item, room not ready, key/access issue.

**Likely shape:** report problem -> explain detail/impact -> answer question -> hear solution -> accept OR ask for one alternative -> confirm.

**B1 boundary:** keep the complaint practical and the resolution space small; tactful escalation and compensation negotiation belong above B1.

**Listening Preview:** optional/recommended.

---

### B1-TT-04 — Re-plan after missing a connection

**Goal:** explain what happened, ask about realistic onward options, compare a small number of consequences, and choose a new route/plan.

**Communication problem:** `short past explanation + information gathering + re-plan`

**Likely shape:** report missed connection -> explain current destination/timing -> hear two options -> ask time/platform/detail -> choose -> confirm next steps.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 changes a normal booking detail. B1 re-plans because an unexpected event already disrupted the trip.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

## Work & Study — 3 missions

### B1-WS-01 — Resolve a work or study misunderstanding ★ Core

**Goal:** explain a misunderstanding about a familiar task/deadline/responsibility, compare what each person understood, and agree the correct next step.

**Communication problem:** `explain misunderstanding + factual discussion + solution`

**Likely shape:** mismatch becomes clear -> learner explains understanding -> partner explains theirs -> check source/detail -> learner asks/answers follow-up -> agree action -> confirm.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 clarifies instructions before acting. B1 repairs a misunderstanding after different interpretations have already formed.

**Listening Preview:** optional/recommended.

---

### B1-WS-02 — Explain a deadline problem and agree a next step

**Goal:** explain why a familiar task may not be completed as planned, give relevant progress/reason, and agree a realistic immediate next step.

**Communication problem:** `progress explanation + problem + solution discussion`

**Likely shape:** give status -> explain obstacle/reason -> answer detail question -> partner proposes or asks for solution -> learner responds/proposes next step -> confirm.

**B1 boundary:** one main obstacle; no negotiation among multiple project priorities or stakeholder interests.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

---

### B1-WS-03 — Give a progress update and answer follow-up questions

**Goal:** give a connected update on a familiar project/course/task, explain what is done, what remains, and one issue or next action.

**Communication problem:** `connected factual update + explanation + follow-up`

**Likely shape:** overall update -> completed work -> current issue/next step -> partner asks follow-up -> learner explains -> confirm action.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 describes routine responsibilities. B1 explains changing progress and a current issue in connected speech.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

## Home & Services — 3 missions

### B1-HS-01 — Explain a recurring service problem and arrange a solution ★ Core

**Goal:** explain that a familiar service problem has happened repeatedly or not been fixed, provide a short history, and agree the next practical action.

**Communication problem:** `recurring problem + short history + solution`

**Likely shape:** report recurring issue -> explain previous occurrence/action -> answer diagnostic detail -> partner proposes visit/reset/replacement -> learner checks suitability -> confirm.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 describes a current symptom and books help. B1 explains persistence/history, making the context necessary to choose the next step.

**Authoring note:** avoid technical expertise requirements and adversarial blame.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### B1-HS-02 — Follow up on a delayed service or delivery

**Goal:** explain that an expected service/delivery has not happened, give the relevant timeline, ask what happened, and request a concrete update/action.

**Communication problem:** `follow-up complaint + timeline + request action`

**Likely shape:** identify order/service -> explain expected vs actual timeline -> partner checks status -> hear explanation -> request next action/time -> confirm.

**B1 boundary:** no compensation bargaining or formal escalation strategy.

**Listening Preview:** optional/recommended.

---

### B1-HS-03 — Discuss repair options after a diagnosis

**Goal:** understand a simple explanation of what is wrong, ask about two practical repair options, and decide on a next step based on basic needs.

**Communication problem:** `understand explanation + ask consequences + choose solution`

**Likely shape:** technician explains issue -> learner asks what option means -> hear time/cost/basic consequence -> ask about alternative -> choose or schedule -> confirm.

**B1 boundary:** only familiar non-safety-critical household problems and a small number of options. Complex cost/risk trade-offs belong at B2.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

## Plans & Leisure — 3 missions

### B1-PL-01 — Re-plan an outing when something changes ★ Core

**Goal:** adapt an agreed outing after a meaningful but familiar change such as weather, closure, delay, or one person's availability.

**Communication problem:** `changed condition + propose solutions + agree revised plan`

**Likely shape:** learn change -> react/explain impact -> propose alternative -> partner reacts/proposes another -> compare briefly -> agree revised time/place/activity -> confirm.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 handles one simple alternative while making a plan. B1 revises an already agreed plan because a new complication appears.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### B1-PL-02 — Choose between activities and justify the decision

**Goal:** discuss two or three familiar activity options, express opinions, give brief reasons, respond to another person's preference, and reach a decision.

**Communication problem:** `opinion + reasons + collaborative decision`

**Likely shape:** review options -> learner preference/reason -> partner preference -> learner responds -> ask/check practical detail -> agree choice.

**B1 boundary:** ordinary collaborative decision, not strategic negotiation among competing constraints.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

---

### B1-PL-03 — Solve a booking or availability problem for an activity

**Goal:** handle a familiar leisure booking problem such as sold-out time, changed session, or booking not found and find a workable alternative.

**Communication problem:** `booking problem + explanation + alternative branch`

**Likely shape:** identify booking/request -> discover issue -> explain need/timing -> partner offers alternative -> learner asks one/two details -> accept or ask another option -> confirm.

**A2 -> B1 progression:** A2 asks about an activity and decides whether to join. B1 continues effectively after the expected booking path fails.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

## What B1 deliberately leaves for B2+

B1 should generally not require:

- negotiating several competing constraints at once
- sustained polite disagreement
- challenging another person's proposal strategically
- defending a complex position over several exchanges
- sophisticated register shifts
- relationship-sensitive criticism
- ambiguous stakeholder motives
- mediation between competing positions
- high-stakes persuasion

B1 can **solve a problem**. B2 begins to require **strategy and trade-offs while solving it**.

# Cross-level progression checks from A2

```text
A2 targeted clarification
-> B1 explain and repair an actual misunderstanding

A2 short past activity exchange
-> B1 connected account with event + reaction/result

A2 order with preferences
-> B1 resolve a wrong order / complain after service failure

A2 compare normal travel options
-> B1 recover after a disruption

A2 change one booking detail from available choices
-> B1 requested change unavailable; accept/reject an alternative

A2 describe current service problem and arrange help
-> B1 explain recurring history and choose a solution path

A2 arrange with one simple availability constraint
-> B1 re-plan after a meaningful new complication

A2 guided connected social exchange
-> B1 enter and sustain an unprepared familiar conversation with learner-owned follow-ups
```

If a B1 mission can be completed with only A2-style isolated detail exchange and no meaningful independence/complication, it is probably mis-leveled.
