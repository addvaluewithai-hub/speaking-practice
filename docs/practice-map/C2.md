# C2 Practice Map

Status: **first full map drafted**

This is a level library map, not a required lesson sequence.

`Core` means **Great place to start**, not prerequisite.

## Design boundary

C2 Practice should test **exceptional flexibility and precision in plausible human interaction**, not obscure vocabulary, trivia, or artificial intellectual puzzles.

Typical C2 mission shape:

- several layers of meaning, goal, or relationship may operate at once
- positions can be partly implicit, deliberately softened, or reframed during the conversation
- the learner must preserve subtle distinctions while reformulating
- register/stance may need to shift rapidly as roles or audience change
- mediation may involve several legitimate but differently framed interests
- irony, allusion, humour, or indirect language may matter when natural to the scenario
- the learner can reshape the interaction itself rather than merely respond to it
- there may be no single ideal solution; success is flexible, precise communication toward a defensible outcome

C2 missions must remain realistic and purposeful. The learner should feel like they are handling a demanding human situation, not solving a linguistic riddle.

## Shared-English source anchors

Detailed language grounding is selected when a mission is promoted to a full contract. The C2 map is informed by reviewed C2 abilities in `addvaluewithai-hub/english-course`, including:

- `ability.cefr_all_descriptors.cefr.cv2020.p138.c2.flexibility.001` — reformulate ideas with great flexibility to emphasise, differentiate by situation/interlocutor, and eliminate ambiguity
- `ability.cefr_all_descriptors.cefr.cv2020.p054.c2.overall_reading_comprehension.002` — appreciate subtle distinctions of style and implicit as well as explicit meaning
- `ability.cefr_all_descriptors.cefr.cv2020.p125.c2.building_on_pluricultural_repertoire.001` — make subtle contextual/cultural adjustments to prevent or repair misunderstandings
- `ability.cefr_all_descriptors.cefr.cv2020.p113.c2.managing_interaction.001` — take on different interaction roles, including mediator, according to participant needs
- `ability.cefr_all_descriptors.cefr.cv2020.p085.c2.online_conversation_and_discussion.001` — adjust language flexibly and sensitively to context, including emotional, allusive, and joking usage

These are design anchors, not mandatory item checks.

## Core / Great place to start

One Core mission per World:

1. `C2-EV-01` — Resolve a layered dispute without flattening the nuance
2. `C2-PS-01` — Mediate an entrenched personal disagreement
3. `C2-FS-01` — Negotiate a complex service resolution across policy and fairness
4. `C2-TT-01` — Resolve a multi-provider travel breakdown
5. `C2-WS-01` — Mediate a high-stakes professional disagreement
6. `C2-HS-01` — Resolve a multi-party responsibility dispute
7. `C2-PL-01` — Mediate a group decision with hidden priorities

Listening Preview is **rare/selective** at C2. Use it only when there is a clear product reason; uncertainty and spontaneous interpretation are often part of the mission difficulty.

# Mission library

## Everyday Life — 3 missions

### C2-EV-01 — Resolve a layered dispute without flattening the nuance ★ Core

**Goal:** resolve a familiar but complex dispute where facts, responsibility, intentions, and face-saving concerns are intertwined and different participants frame the same issue differently.

**Communication problem:** `layered dispute + reframing + negotiated resolution`

**Likely shape:** hear one framing -> second framing challenges assumptions -> learner separates facts/interpretations/interests -> reformulates each fairly -> identifies hidden incompatibility/common ground -> proposes route -> adapts after new information -> summarises precise outcome.

**C1 -> C2 progression:** C1 handles ambiguity tactfully. C2 must preserve several nuanced interpretations at once and reformulate them without distorting anyone's position.

**Listening Preview:** generally unnecessary.

---

### C2-EV-02 — Reformulate a complex position as the audience changes

**Goal:** communicate one nuanced practical position across an informal peer, a formal decision-maker, and/or a frustrated participant, shifting emphasis/register while preserving the underlying meaning.

**Communication problem:** `rapid audience adaptation + precise reformulation`

**Likely shape:** explain position in one register -> new interlocutor enters with different assumptions -> restate/reframe -> challenged on apparent inconsistency -> clarify distinction -> adapt again -> reach aligned understanding.

**C2 difficulty:** the learner must vary form and emphasis without accidentally changing the substantive position.

**Listening Preview:** unnecessary by default.

---

### C2-EV-03 — Respond to indirect or ironic criticism without misreading it

**Goal:** recognise a criticism/concern expressed indirectly, humorously, or ironically, test the interpretation tactfully, and respond at the appropriate level of seriousness.

**Communication problem:** `implicit meaning + tone calibration + repair`

**Likely shape:** partner makes indirect/allusive comment -> learner responds without overcommitting to one reading -> probes/acknowledges -> partner clarifies or doubles down -> learner adjusts tone -> addresses real issue -> close.

**Authoring rule:** cues must be fair and interpretable in the authored context. Do not require culture-specific trivia or mind-reading.

**Listening Preview:** no.

## People & Social — 3 missions

### C2-PS-01 — Mediate an entrenched personal disagreement ★ Core

**Goal:** help two people who have repeated, partly incompatible narratives of a conflict understand the other's underlying concerns and reach a workable next step.

**Communication problem:** `deep mediation + perspective translation + face management`

**Likely shape:** each side presents position -> learner identifies different meanings attached to same event -> asks questions that expose needs/assumptions -> reframes without taking over -> handles emotional resistance -> proposes common frame -> facilitates solution -> precisely summarises what is and is not agreed.

**C1 -> C2 progression:** C1 mediates ambiguity diplomatically. C2 manages layered narratives and preserves subtle distinctions between intention, impact, principle, and practical need.

**Listening Preview:** generally unnecessary.

---

### C2-PS-02 — Navigate a socially delicate conversation with shifting tone

**Goal:** handle a conversation that moves between humour, seriousness, indirect criticism, and genuine concern without losing track of the relationship or intended meaning.

**Communication problem:** `tone shifts + implication + social flexibility`

**Likely shape:** light opening -> joking/allusive comment carries concern -> learner responds in matching tone -> issue becomes explicit -> learner shifts register appropriately -> clarifies boundaries/position -> returns to stable social close.

**C2 boundary:** complexity comes from linguistic/pragmatic flexibility, not emotional manipulation.

**Listening Preview:** no.

---

### C2-PS-03 — Repair a cross-style or cross-cultural misunderstanding

**Goal:** recognise that different communication conventions/styles contributed to a misunderstanding, explain the mismatch without stereotyping, and establish a shared way to continue.

**Communication problem:** `meta-communication + subtle adjustment + repair`

**Likely shape:** misunderstanding appears -> learner tests interpretation -> identifies difference in directness/formality/expectation -> explains own intention -> validates other reading -> adjusts expression -> agrees clearer convention/next step.

**Authoring rule:** use fictional individual preferences/communication norms; never require essentialist claims about cultures or groups.

**Listening Preview:** optional only if the exemplar itself is pedagogically justified.

## Food & Shopping — 3 missions

### C2-FS-01 — Negotiate a complex service resolution across policy and fairness ★ Core

**Goal:** resolve a service failure where formal policy, prior representations, practical impact, and fairness point in partly different directions.

**Communication problem:** `policy + fairness + precedent + nuanced negotiation`

**Likely shape:** establish detailed history -> provider invokes policy -> learner distinguishes literal rule from case-specific expectation -> provider raises consistency/precedent concern -> learner reframes request proportionately -> explore solutions with different implications -> reach exception/compromise/escalation outcome.

**C1 -> C2 progression:** C1 argues within ambiguous policy. C2 must manage several legitimate principles and reformulate the case as the provider's framing changes.

**Authoring note:** fictional policy; no real legal advice.

**Listening Preview:** generally unnecessary.

---

### C2-FS-02 — Handle a sophisticated complaint where the other side needs a face-saving path

**Goal:** secure a meaningful correction after a serious service issue while allowing the provider to acknowledge/fix the problem without forcing an unnecessarily confrontational admission.

**Communication problem:** `complaint + implication + face-saving solution design`

**Likely shape:** state problem/evidence -> provider uses cautious/defensive language -> learner distinguishes outcome from blame -> signals unacceptable gap -> offers framing that enables correction -> provider shifts position -> negotiate details -> close precisely.

**C2 difficulty:** learner manages both substantive outcome and the discourse route that makes agreement possible.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

---

### C2-FS-03 — Negotiate bespoke conditions without losing critical distinctions

**Goal:** specify a non-standard product/service requirement, respond to proposed substitutions that are almost-but-not-quite equivalent, and negotiate conditions while preserving the distinctions that matter.

**Communication problem:** `fine distinctions + reformulation + condition negotiation`

**Likely shape:** explain nuanced requirement -> provider paraphrases -> learner corrects subtle difference -> provider proposes near-equivalent option -> learner explains consequence -> adjusts one requirement but preserves another -> final precise confirmation.

**C1 -> C2 progression:** C1 repairs complex assumptions. C2 repeatedly reformulates subtle distinctions as the solution itself evolves.

**Listening Preview:** no.

## Travel & Transport — 3 missions

### C2-TT-01 — Resolve a multi-provider travel breakdown ★ Core

**Goal:** secure a workable outcome when several providers or departments each own part of a complex disruption and their responsibilities/terminology do not align neatly.

**Communication problem:** `multi-party systems + reframing + escalation + coordination`

**Likely shape:** provider A frames issue narrowly -> learner reconstructs chain -> provider B's incompatible framing appears -> learner translates between terms/responsibilities -> distinguishes immediate need from later liability -> redirects conversation to actionable authority -> coordinates solution -> summarises commitments.

**C1 -> C2 progression:** C1 handles two-provider ambiguity. C2 coordinates multiple frames and roles dynamically without losing precision.

**Listening Preview:** no by default.

---

### C2-TT-02 — Advocate for an unusual need across changing authority levels

**Goal:** explain an unusual but non-medical/non-safety-critical travel need to frontline staff, specialist staff, and a decision-maker, adapting the case while preserving essential requirements.

**Communication problem:** `role shifts + register shifts + strategic reformulation`

**Likely shape:** initial request -> frontline constraint -> transfer -> technical clarification -> decision-maker focuses on policy/impact -> learner reframes needs/objectives -> negotiate feasible exception/process -> confirm exact conditions.

**C2 difficulty:** each interlocutor needs a different framing; superficial repetition should fail to move the interaction.

**Listening Preview:** no.

---

### C2-TT-03 — Mediate a group travel conflict shaped by hidden assumptions

**Goal:** help a group resolve a travel-planning conflict where stated preferences mask different assumptions about fairness, comfort, obligation, or shared experience.

**Communication problem:** `implicit priorities + mediation + group reframing`

**Likely shape:** explicit argument over option -> learner probes reasons -> uncovers different underlying assumptions -> reframes conflict -> tests common principles -> proposes options in new frame -> participants revise positions -> summarise agreement/remaining difference.

**Listening Preview:** generally unnecessary.

## Work & Study — 3 missions

### C2-WS-01 — Mediate a high-stakes professional disagreement ★ Core

**Goal:** facilitate a demanding workplace/academic disagreement where parties have different evidence, priorities, status, and definitions of success, without reducing the conflict to a false simple compromise.

**Communication problem:** `layered professional mediation + precision + role management`

**Likely shape:** positions stated -> learner clarifies terms/criteria -> detects parties using same words differently -> reframes evidence/needs -> handles challenge to neutrality/process -> explores conditional agreement -> separates settled/unsettled points -> defines next decision/action.

**C1 -> C2 progression:** C1 facilitates conflicting priorities/unclear goals. C2 mediates competing frameworks themselves and adapts role as the interaction changes.

**Listening Preview:** no.

---

### C2-WS-02 — Reframe a stalled negotiation when both sides use different assumptions

**Goal:** restart a professional negotiation that has stalled because the parties frame value, risk, timing, or responsibility differently.

**Communication problem:** `meta-negotiation + conceptual reframing + conditional agreement`

**Likely shape:** repeated positions fail -> learner identifies mismatch in assumptions -> proposes shared definitions/decision criteria -> tests each side's real limit -> reformulates offer in new frame -> handle objection -> build conditional agreement.

**C2 boundary:** scenario supplies all domain facts; no specialised legal/business expertise should be required.

**Listening Preview:** no.

---

### C2-WS-03 — Respond to diplomatically coded criticism and reposition your case

**Goal:** recognise criticism/concern expressed through highly softened professional language, clarify what is actually at stake, and adjust/defend a position without overreacting or pretending the criticism was not made.

**Communication problem:** `implicit professional meaning + stance calibration + precise response`

**Likely shape:** senior/peer offers coded critique -> learner acknowledges likely concern carefully -> asks precise question -> receives partial confirmation -> distinguishes points to concede vs defend -> reformulates proposal -> confirm expectations.

**C1 -> C2 progression:** C1 handles direct but sensitive feedback. C2 reliably works with highly indirect signalling and preserves nuanced stance.

**Listening Preview:** no.

## Home & Services — 3 missions

### C2-HS-01 — Resolve a multi-party responsibility dispute ★ Core

**Goal:** move a complex service/property issue forward when owner/provider/occupant or multiple contractors each define responsibility differently and no party controls the full solution.

**Communication problem:** `multi-party responsibility + systems mediation + actionable agreement`

**Likely shape:** collect positions -> distinguish technical cause/contractual responsibility/immediate need as separate questions -> translate between parties -> challenge circular referrals -> identify authority for each action -> negotiate interim + final paths -> summarise commitments precisely.

**C1 -> C2 progression:** C1 handles uncertain causation between parties. C2 manages several interacting responsibility frames without collapsing them.

**Authoring note:** fictional, non-legal outcome; no real rights/advice claims.

**Listening Preview:** no.

---

### C2-HS-02 — Negotiate complex service terms expressed in deliberately soft/vague language

**Goal:** clarify what a provider is actually committing to when important conditions are expressed indirectly or ambiguously, and negotiate wording/expectations until both sides share a precise interpretation.

**Communication problem:** `strategic ambiguity + clarification + precise agreement`

**Likely shape:** provider offers vague assurance -> learner probes scope/timing/conditions -> provider softens/qualifies -> learner paraphrases implication -> corrects misunderstanding -> negotiates exact commitment -> confirms limits.

**C2 difficulty:** preserving nuances between guarantee, intention, possibility, and condition.

**Listening Preview:** no.

---

### C2-HS-03 — Detect an implicit refusal and redesign the request

**Goal:** recognise when a service provider is effectively refusing without saying “no” directly, identify the real constraint, and reformulate the request into something actionable.

**Communication problem:** `implicit refusal + inference + strategic reformulation`

**Likely shape:** request -> provider gives indirect obstacles -> learner tests whether issue is policy/capacity/scope -> provider clarifies partially -> learner reframes narrower/different request -> provider responds -> negotiate exact next step.

**Authoring rule:** the authored conversation must contain enough evidence for the inference; no arbitrary guessing.

**Listening Preview:** no.

## Plans & Leisure — 3 missions

### C2-PL-01 — Mediate a group decision with hidden priorities ★ Core

**Goal:** guide a group toward a plan when stated options are only the surface issue and participants have partly hidden concerns about fairness, inclusion, status, effort, or previous commitments.

**Communication problem:** `implicit interests + group mediation + adaptive framing`

**Likely shape:** group argues options -> learner notices repeated non-resolution -> probes underlying criteria -> participants reveal different concerns -> learner reframes decision around principles -> generates/elicits options -> tests acceptability -> summarises nuanced agreement.

**C1 -> C2 progression:** C1 uncovers implicit priorities. C2 handles several simultaneous hidden/partly conflicting priorities and adapts the decision frame itself.

**Listening Preview:** no.

---

### C2-PL-02 — Rescue a socially delicate event plan after an awkward change

**Goal:** repair an event/plan after a change has created offence, exclusion, or conflicting expectations, while balancing practical needs and social meaning.

**Communication problem:** `social repair + planning + layered face needs`

**Likely shape:** change announced -> participant reacts indirectly/strongly -> learner identifies social impact -> clarifies practical necessity -> apologises/acknowledges proportionately -> redesigns plan/inclusion -> handles competing reaction -> settle.

**C2 difficulty:** several interpretations of fairness/intent must be managed without formulaic apologies.

**Listening Preview:** optional only if testing a specific pragmatic pattern.

---

### C2-PL-03 — Reframe conflict between different communication expectations during planning

**Goal:** resolve planning friction caused less by the actual choice and more by different expectations about directness, commitment, spontaneity, or decision-making style.

**Communication problem:** `meta-communication + style mediation + practical resolution`

**Likely shape:** planning repeatedly stalls -> learner identifies communication-process mismatch -> articulates both styles neutrally -> proposes temporary shared convention -> re-runs decision -> notices residual misunderstanding -> adjusts -> final plan.

**Authoring rule:** individuals have styles; do not essentialise cultures, generations, genders, or other groups.

**Listening Preview:** no.

## C2 anti-patterns

Do not create C2 missions by:

- inserting rare words for their own sake
- demanding academic/philosophical knowledge
- making Gemini deliberately obscure without contextual reason
- relying on culture-specific trivia
- forcing impossible “read their mind” inference
- turning every mission into formal workplace negotiation
- equating longer speaking with higher proficiency

C2 should demonstrate **flexibility, precision, inference, reformulation, and role-sensitive control** in situations that advanced speakers could plausibly face.

# Cross-level progression checks from C1

```text
C1 manage ambiguity diplomatically
-> C2 preserve/reconcile several nuanced interpretations while the framing itself changes

C1 adapt register to audience
-> C2 shift audience/role/stance rapidly without changing substantive meaning

C1 mediate a relationship-sensitive misunderstanding
-> C2 mediate entrenched layered narratives and explicitly manage the interaction process

C1 argue a complex case precisely
-> C2 reformulate that case repeatedly as new assumptions/frames emerge

C1 recognise implicit concern
-> C2 calibrate response to irony, allusion, softened criticism, or strategic ambiguity

C1 facilitate conflicting priorities
-> C2 redesign the shared decision frame when parties are operating from incompatible assumptions
```

If a C2 mission is simply “C1 but longer/more formal,” it should be rewritten.
