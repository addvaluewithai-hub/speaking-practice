# C2 Practice Map

Status: **first full map reviewed and Core rebalanced**

This is a library map, not a required lesson sequence. `Core` means **Great place to start**, not prerequisite.

## Design boundary

C2 tests **exceptional flexibility and precision in plausible human interaction**, not rare vocabulary, trivia, or artificial intellectual puzzles.

Typical shape:

- several layers of meaning, goal, relationship, or framing may operate at once
- positions can be partly implicit, softened, or reframed during the interaction
- learner preserves subtle distinctions while reformulating
- register/stance may shift quickly as audience or role changes
- mediation can involve several legitimate but differently framed interests
- irony, allusion, humour, or strategic indirectness may matter when natural to the situation
- learner can redesign the interaction frame, not merely respond inside it

There may be no single ideal solution. Success is flexible, precise communication toward a defensible outcome.

## Shared-English source anchors

Mission-specific grounding is selected when a mission is promoted. Map design is informed by reviewed C2 abilities such as:

- reformulating ideas flexibly to emphasise, differentiate by interlocutor, and eliminate ambiguity
- appreciating subtle distinctions of style and implicit/explicit meaning
- making subtle contextual adjustments to prevent/repair misunderstanding
- changing interaction role according to participant needs, including mediation
- adapting language sensitively to emotional, allusive, and joking usage

These are design anchors, not learner item checklists.

## Core / Great place to start

The first draft over-selected disputes/mediation. The reviewed C2 Core demonstrates a broader sample of exceptional flexibility:

1. `C2-EV-02` — Reformulate a complex position as the audience changes — `rapid audience adaptation`
2. `C2-PS-02` — Navigate a socially delicate conversation with shifting tone — `tone / implication / social flexibility`
3. `C2-FS-03` — Negotiate bespoke conditions without losing critical distinctions — `fine distinctions / reformulation`
4. `C2-TT-02` — Advocate for an unusual need across changing authority levels — `role / register / strategic reframing`
5. `C2-WS-01` — Mediate a high-stakes professional disagreement — `layered mediation`
6. `C2-HS-02` — Negotiate complex service terms expressed in soft/vague language — `strategic ambiguity / precision`
7. `C2-PL-03` — Reframe conflict between different communication expectations during planning — `meta-communication / style mediation`

The library still contains substantial dispute/mediation practice, but the entry set no longer equates “advanced English” with conflict management.

Listening Preview is rare/selective at C2. Uncertainty and spontaneous interpretation are often part of the mission difficulty.

# Mission library

## Everyday Life — 3

### C2-EV-01 — Resolve a layered dispute without flattening the nuance
**Goal:** separate facts, interpretations, interests, and face-saving concerns across different versions of a complex everyday dispute, then build a workable resolution.
**Problem:** `layered dispute + reframing + negotiated resolution`
**Progression:** C1 manages ambiguity tactfully; C2 preserves several nuanced interpretations while the framing itself evolves.

### C2-EV-02 — Reformulate a complex position as the audience changes ★ Core
**Goal:** communicate one nuanced position to different interlocutors, changing form/emphasis/register without accidentally changing the substantive meaning.
**Problem:** `rapid audience adaptation + precise reformulation`
**C2 difficulty:** a superficial repeat is not enough; each audience needs a different framing.

### C2-EV-03 — Respond to indirect or ironic criticism without misreading it
**Goal:** recognise criticism/concern expressed indirectly, humorously, or ironically, test the interpretation tactfully, and calibrate the response appropriately.
**Problem:** `implicit meaning + tone calibration + repair`
**Guardrail:** authored cues must be fair and contextual; no culture-trivia or mind-reading.

## People & Social — 3

### C2-PS-01 — Mediate an entrenched personal disagreement
**Goal:** help two people with repeated incompatible narratives distinguish intention, impact, principle, and practical need, then identify a workable next step.
**Problem:** `deep mediation + perspective translation + face management`

### C2-PS-02 — Navigate a socially delicate conversation with shifting tone ★ Core
**Goal:** move fluently between humour, seriousness, indirect criticism, and genuine concern without losing the relationship or intended meaning.
**Problem:** `tone shifts + implication + social flexibility`
**Progression:** C1 can handle indirect concern; C2 tracks and responds to repeated pragmatic shifts within the same exchange.

### C2-PS-03 — Repair a cross-style or cross-cultural misunderstanding
**Goal:** identify how different communication expectations contributed to a misunderstanding, explain the mismatch without stereotyping, and establish a clearer shared convention.
**Problem:** `meta-communication + subtle adjustment + repair`
**Guardrail:** characters have individual communication styles; no essentialist claims about groups.

## Food & Shopping — 3

### C2-FS-01 — Negotiate a complex service resolution across policy and fairness
**Goal:** resolve a failure where policy, previous representations, practical impact, fairness, and precedent point in partly different directions.
**Problem:** `policy + fairness + precedent + nuanced negotiation`
**Guardrail:** fictional policy; no real legal advice.

### C2-FS-02 — Handle a sophisticated complaint where the other side needs a face-saving path
**Goal:** secure meaningful correction while creating a discourse route that allows the provider to change position without unnecessary confrontation.
**Problem:** `complaint + implication + face-saving solution design`

### C2-FS-03 — Negotiate bespoke conditions without losing critical distinctions ★ Core
**Goal:** specify a non-standard requirement, reject almost-but-not-quite equivalent paraphrases/substitutions, and negotiate conditions while preserving the distinctions that matter.
**Problem:** `fine distinctions + reformulation + condition negotiation`
**Progression:** C1 repairs complex assumptions; C2 repeatedly reformulates subtle distinctions as the solution itself changes.

## Travel & Transport — 3

### C2-TT-01 — Resolve a multi-provider travel breakdown
**Goal:** coordinate a solution when several providers/departments own different parts of a disruption and use incompatible responsibility/terminology frames.
**Problem:** `multi-party systems + reframing + escalation + coordination`

### C2-TT-02 — Advocate for an unusual need across changing authority levels ★ Core
**Goal:** explain an unusual but non-medical/non-safety-critical need to frontline staff, specialist staff, and a decision-maker, adapting the case while preserving essential requirements.
**Problem:** `role shifts + register shifts + strategic reformulation`
**C2 difficulty:** each interlocutor needs a different framing; repetition alone should not move the interaction.

### C2-TT-03 — Mediate a group travel conflict shaped by hidden assumptions
**Goal:** uncover different assumptions about fairness, comfort, obligation, or shared experience and reframe the group decision around more useful principles.
**Problem:** `implicit priorities + mediation + group reframing`

## Work & Study — 3

### C2-WS-01 — Mediate a high-stakes professional disagreement ★ Core
**Goal:** facilitate a demanding workplace/academic disagreement where parties have different evidence, priorities, status, and definitions of success without reducing it to a false simple compromise.
**Problem:** `layered professional mediation + precision + role management`
**Progression:** C1 facilitates conflicting priorities; C2 mediates competing frameworks and may change interaction role as needed.

### C2-WS-02 — Reframe a stalled negotiation when both sides use different assumptions
**Goal:** restart a negotiation by identifying incompatible assumptions about value, risk, timing, or responsibility and constructing shared decision criteria.
**Problem:** `meta-negotiation + conceptual reframing + conditional agreement`
**Guardrail:** scenario supplies all domain facts; specialist business/legal expertise is not required.

### C2-WS-03 — Respond to diplomatically coded criticism and reposition your case
**Goal:** recognise highly softened professional criticism, clarify what is actually at stake, decide what to concede/defend, and reformulate the proposal precisely.
**Problem:** `implicit professional meaning + stance calibration + precise response`

## Home & Services — 3

### C2-HS-01 — Resolve a multi-party responsibility dispute
**Goal:** move a complex service/property problem forward when several parties define cause, responsibility, and authority differently and no one controls the whole solution.
**Problem:** `multi-party responsibility + systems mediation + actionable agreement`
**Guardrail:** fictional, non-legal outcome; no real rights/advice claims.

### C2-HS-02 — Negotiate complex service terms expressed in deliberately soft/vague language ★ Core
**Goal:** clarify exactly what a provider is committing to when important conditions are indirect/ambiguous, then negotiate wording and expectations until both sides share a precise interpretation.
**Problem:** `strategic ambiguity + clarification + precise agreement`
**C2 difficulty:** preserve differences between guarantee, intention, possibility, condition, and best effort.

### C2-HS-03 — Detect an implicit refusal and redesign the request
**Goal:** recognise when a provider is effectively refusing without saying “no”, identify the real constraint, and reformulate the request into something actionable.
**Problem:** `implicit refusal + inference + strategic reformulation`
**Guardrail:** authored context must contain enough evidence for the inference.

## Plans & Leisure — 3

### C2-PL-01 — Mediate a group decision with hidden priorities
**Goal:** guide a group when the stated options mask concerns about fairness, inclusion, status, effort, or previous commitments, and redesign the decision frame around those interests.
**Problem:** `implicit interests + group mediation + adaptive framing`

### C2-PL-02 — Rescue a socially delicate event plan after an awkward change
**Goal:** repair a plan after a change creates offence/exclusion/conflicting expectations while balancing practical needs, intent, and face needs.
**Problem:** `social repair + planning + layered face management`

### C2-PL-03 — Reframe conflict between different communication expectations during planning ★ Core
**Goal:** resolve planning friction caused less by the option itself than by different expectations about directness, commitment, spontaneity, or how decisions should be made.
**Problem:** `meta-communication + style mediation + practical resolution`
**Progression:** C1 manages partly implicit priorities; C2 can identify and change the interaction process itself.
**Guardrail:** individual styles only; do not essentialise cultures, generations, genders, or other groups.

## C2 anti-patterns

Do not create C2 difficulty by:

- inserting rare words for their own sake
- demanding academic/philosophical knowledge
- making Gemini deliberately obscure without contextual reason
- relying on culture-specific trivia
- forcing impossible inference
- turning every mission into formal workplace negotiation
- equating longer speaking with higher proficiency

C2 should demonstrate **flexibility, precision, inference, reformulation, and role-sensitive control** in situations advanced speakers could plausibly face.

If a C2 mission is simply “C1 but longer/more formal”, rewrite it.
