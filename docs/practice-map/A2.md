# A2 Practice Map

Status: **first full map drafted**

This is a level library map, not a required lesson sequence.

`Core` means **Great place to start**, not prerequisite.

## Design boundary

A2 remains routine and familiar, but the learner should do more than complete one isolated exchange.

Typical A2 mission shape:

- one main real-world goal plus one secondary detail
- roughly 4–7 meaningful learner moves
- short but slightly more connected turns than A1
- simple preferences, comparisons, reasons, and follow-up questions
- one bounded variation or minor complication where useful
- moderate predictability
- cooperative or neutral interlocutor
- dynamic hints available, but the mission should be independently manageable for an A2 learner

A2 should not require extended storytelling, multi-step negotiation, complaint escalation, sustained disagreement, or management of multiple competing constraints. Those belong mainly to B1+.

## Core / Great place to start

These eight missions demonstrate the main A2 progression from A1 across all seven worlds:

1. `A2-EV-01` — Clarify a short message or plan
2. `A2-PS-01` — Get to know someone better
3. `A2-FS-01` — Order with preferences
4. `A2-TT-01` — Ask for directions and clarify the route
5. `A2-TT-02` — Change a simple travel booking detail
6. `A2-WS-02` — Clarify task instructions
7. `A2-HS-01` — Describe a simple service problem and arrange help
8. `A2-PL-01` — Plan an outing with preferences

Listening Preview is **recommended** for A2 Core missions, not a publication requirement by default. It remains optional for the learner.

# Mission library

## Everyday Life — 4 missions

### A2-EV-01 — Clarify a short message or plan ★ Core

**Goal:** recover when one part of a short routine message/plan was unclear, ask specifically about the missing detail, and confirm the final information.

**Communication problem:** `targeted clarification + confirm`

**Likely shape:** hear short message -> identify unclear part -> ask targeted clarification -> hear answer -> confirm key details -> close.

**A1 -> A2 progression:** A1 asks for simple repetition. A2 identifies what is unclear and checks the meaning/detail rather than simply replaying the whole turn.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### A2-EV-02 — Describe your routine and compare schedules

**Goal:** describe a familiar daily routine, answer one or two follow-ups, and notice one simple difference or similarity with another person's routine.

**Communication problem:** `describe routine + follow-up + simple comparison`

**Likely shape:** describe routine -> answer when/how often -> hear partner routine -> compare one point -> react/close.

**Authoring note:** keep the comparison concrete: start time, commute, lunch, study/work hours, exercise, or bedtime. Do not require a long monologue.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

---

### A2-EV-03 — Report a lost item and describe it

**Goal:** report one familiar lost item, describe it simply, say where/when it was last seen, and understand the next step.

**Communication problem:** `state problem + describe + past detail + follow instruction`

**Likely shape:** report lost item -> describe colour/size/type -> say last place/time -> answer one follow-up -> receive next step -> confirm.

**A2 boundary:** the item/location facts stay simple and known. No police report, insurance claim, or complex reconstruction.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### A2-EV-04 — Confirm details on a short phone call

**Goal:** make or receive a predictable short call about an arrival time, meeting detail, or simple update and confirm the important information.

**Communication problem:** `phone interaction + information exchange + repair`

**Likely shape:** identify reason for call -> exchange one/two details -> ask to repeat/confirm if needed -> final confirmation -> close.

**Authoring note:** the topic must be predictable and concrete. Unknown-caller problem solving belongs above A2.

**Listening Preview:** recommended because the audio-only interaction pattern is useful to model.

## People & Social — 3 missions

### A2-PS-01 — Get to know someone better ★ Core

**Goal:** move beyond name/basic identity and exchange simple information about routine, interests, preferences, or recent activities with natural reciprocal questions.

**Communication problem:** `social follow-up + reciprocal exchange + preference`

**Likely shape:** opening -> ask interest/routine question -> answer -> one follow-up -> ask back -> simple reaction/reason -> close.

**A1 -> A2 progression:** A1 handles basic introductions. A2 sustains a short familiar conversation with related follow-ups.

**Privacy rule:** real personal disclosure is never required; fictional/chosen information remains valid.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### A2-PS-02 — Catch up about a recent weekend

**Goal:** ask and answer a few simple questions about a recent weekend/activity and give a brief reaction or evaluation.

**Communication problem:** `past activity + follow-up + reaction`

**Likely shape:** ask what happened/did -> short answer -> when/where/who follow-up -> say how it was -> reciprocal question -> close.

**Authoring note:** keep narration to a few connected facts. A detailed story with sequence, complication, and resolution belongs at B1+.

**Listening Preview:** optional/recommended.

---

### A2-PS-03 — Recommend a familiar place to a friend

**Goal:** recommend one familiar café, shop, park, place, or activity and give one or two simple reasons that match the friend's need.

**Communication problem:** `recommend + simple reason + answer follow-up`

**Likely shape:** friend states simple need -> recommend option -> give reason -> answer one detail such as location/price/time -> friend reacts -> close.

**A2 boundary:** no comparison of many competing criteria. One clear recommendation with simple support is enough.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

## Food & Shopping — 4 missions

### A2-FS-01 — Order with preferences ★ Core

**Goal:** order food/drink, state one or two simple preferences, answer a short follow-up, and complete the transaction.

**Communication problem:** `request + preference + bounded follow-up + transaction`

**Likely shape:** order -> state preference -> partner checks one detail -> confirm/change one simple option -> price/payment -> close.

**Possible preferences:** hot/cold, small/large, with/without a familiar ingredient, here/takeaway, simple side choice.

**A1 -> A2 progression:** A1 makes a straightforward request. A2 shapes the request with preferences and handles one extra service decision.

**Safety/authoring note:** do not require medical/allergy disclosure. Ingredient preferences should be ordinary product choices unless a future mission is explicitly designed for a different domain.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### A2-FS-02 — Ask about menu options and choose

**Goal:** ask one or two simple questions about available menu options, compare basic differences, and choose.

**Communication problem:** `ask information + compare + choose`

**Likely shape:** ask what's available -> ask one detail such as size/content/price -> hear two options -> compare simply -> choose/order -> close.

**A2 boundary:** keep menu truth small and concrete; no negotiation, complaint, or dietary-medical complexity.

**Listening Preview:** optional/recommended.

---

### A2-FS-03 — Compare two products and choose

**Goal:** ask about two familiar products, compare a few basic features, state a preference/reason, and choose one.

**Communication problem:** `ask information + compare + preference + transaction`

**Likely shape:** ask about product A/B -> hear price/size/basic feature -> compare -> state preference/reason -> choose/buy -> close.

**Possible products:** bags, headphones, shoes, simple household items, tickets/passes, or familiar everyday goods.

**A2 boundary:** two options and a small number of criteria only. Trade-offs among multiple constraints belong at B2.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

---

### A2-FS-04 — Exchange an item for a simple reason

**Goal:** explain one clear problem with a recently purchased item such as wrong size/colour, ask for an exchange, and accept one straightforward offered solution.

**Communication problem:** `state simple problem + request change + respond to option`

**Likely shape:** state purchase/problem -> give simple reason -> ask exchange -> partner offers one valid replacement -> accept/confirm -> close.

**A1 -> A2 progression:** A1 buys/selects. A2 handles one predictable after-purchase change.

**Authoring note:** no refund-policy argument, blame, compensation, or complaint escalation. Those are B1/B2 territory.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

## Travel & Transport — 4 missions

### A2-TT-01 — Ask for directions and clarify the route ★ Core

**Goal:** ask how to get to a familiar destination, understand a short multi-step route, clarify one point, and confirm the destination/landmark.

**Communication problem:** `ask directions + follow short sequence + clarification`

**Likely shape:** ask route -> hear two/three simple steps -> ask clarification about turn/landmark -> confirm -> thank.

**A1 -> A2 progression:** A1 locates a nearby place with a very short answer. A2 follows a small route and repairs one unclear step.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### A2-TT-02 — Change a simple travel booking detail ★ Core

**Goal:** change one straightforward booking/ticket detail such as date, time, or seat, hear a small set of available alternatives, and confirm the new choice.

**Communication problem:** `request change + bounded options + arrangement`

**Likely shape:** identify booking -> request one change -> partner offers one/two options -> choose -> confirm price/time if relevant -> close.

**A2 boundary:** one change only, with cooperative alternatives. Complex disruption, fees, cancellation disputes, and negotiation belong above A2.

**Privacy rule:** authored booking names/codes are fictional or placeholder data.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### A2-TT-03 — Check into a hotel and make one extra request

**Goal:** complete a routine hotel check-in and make one simple extra request such as Wi-Fi information, an extra towel, breakfast time, or a quiet room if available.

**Communication problem:** `service check-in + request + follow-up`

**Likely shape:** identify booking -> confirm basic detail -> receive room information -> make extra request -> hear response -> confirm/close.

**A1 -> A2 progression:** A1 handles the check-in skeleton. A2 adds a secondary service need without turning it into a service dispute.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### A2-TT-04 — Compare two travel options and choose

**Goal:** ask about two familiar transport options and choose based on simple factors such as time, price, or direct/with-change.

**Communication problem:** `ask information + compare + preference`

**Likely shape:** ask about options -> hear basic details -> ask one follow-up -> compare -> choose and state simple reason -> close/buy.

**A2 boundary:** two clear options and basic criteria. Multi-leg disruption planning belongs at B1+.

**Listening Preview:** optional/recommended.

## Work & Study — 3 missions

### A2-WS-01 — Explain your routine responsibilities

**Goal:** describe a few familiar work/study responsibilities or a normal day and answer simple follow-up questions about when/how often they happen.

**Communication problem:** `describe routine + give details + follow-up`

**Likely shape:** describe role/routine -> answer time/frequency question -> add one simple preference or difficulty -> ask partner back or close.

**Privacy rule:** learner may use fictional/chosen role or study information.

**Listening Preview:** optional.

---

### A2-WS-02 — Clarify task instructions ★ Core

**Goal:** understand a short familiar task, ask about one unclear step/deadline/detail, restate the important instruction, and continue.

**Communication problem:** `instruction understanding + targeted clarification + confirm`

**Likely shape:** receive short task -> ask what/which/when about unclear part -> hear clarification -> confirm/restate -> close/continue.

**A1 -> A2 progression:** A1 asks one concrete task question. A2 follows a small set of instructions and isolates the part that needs clarification.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### A2-WS-03 — Arrange a meeting or study session

**Goal:** arrange a short meeting/study session while handling one simple availability constraint.

**Communication problem:** `arrange + state availability + choose alternative`

**Likely shape:** suggest time -> one person cannot -> offer/receive one alternative -> agree duration/place -> confirm -> close.

**A2 boundary:** one simple conflict only. Negotiating multiple priorities or deadlines belongs at B1/B2.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

## Home & Services — 3 missions

### A2-HS-01 — Describe a simple service problem and arrange help ★ Core

**Goal:** describe one familiar household/service problem, answer one or two basic diagnostic questions, and arrange the next practical step.

**Communication problem:** `describe problem + answer clarification + arrange help`

**Likely shape:** report problem -> describe symptom -> answer when/what happens -> partner offers simple next step/visit -> confirm time/action -> close.

**Possible problems:** Wi-Fi not working, water not hot, key/access issue, light/appliance not working, delivery not arrived yet.

**A1 -> A2 progression:** A1 states a visible problem and asks for help. A2 adds simple details and handles a routine next-step arrangement.

**Authoring note:** do not require technical troubleshooting, blame, compensation, or complex complaint handling.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### A2-HS-02 — Change or cancel a simple appointment

**Goal:** explain that an existing routine appointment no longer works, change/cancel it, choose from a small set of alternatives, and confirm the new status.

**Communication problem:** `change arrangement + simple reason + confirm`

**Likely shape:** identify appointment -> state need to change/cancel -> optional short reason -> hear alternatives -> choose -> confirm -> close.

**A1 -> A2 progression:** A1 books from offered times. A2 modifies an existing arrangement.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### A2-HS-03 — Ask about service options and choose

**Goal:** ask about two routine service options, understand basic differences in price/time/what is included, and choose one.

**Communication problem:** `service enquiry + compare + choose`

**Likely shape:** state need -> hear two options -> ask one detail -> compare -> choose -> confirm next step -> close.

**Possible contexts:** haircut/service package, delivery option, basic repair visit, gym/class pass, cleaning/laundry service.

**A2 boundary:** no hidden terms, contract negotiation, or high-stakes financial/medical decisions.

**Listening Preview:** optional/recommended.

## Plans & Leisure — 3 missions

### A2-PL-01 — Plan an outing with preferences ★ Core

**Goal:** choose what to do, where to go, and when to meet while expressing a simple preference and reason.

**Communication problem:** `suggest + preference + simple reason + arrangement`

**Likely shape:** suggest activity/place -> partner suggests alternative -> state preference/reason -> agree choice -> choose time/place -> confirm.

**A1 -> A2 progression:** A1 agrees a simple plan from small options. A2 contributes a preference/reason and handles one alternative suggestion.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### A2-PL-02 — Decline an invitation and suggest another plan

**Goal:** respond to an invitation, give a short natural reason if unavailable, suggest one alternative time/activity, and agree or close politely.

**Communication problem:** `respond to invitation + simple reason + alternative`

**Likely shape:** receive invitation -> decline/partly accept -> short reason -> suggest alternative -> partner responds -> confirm/close.

**A1 -> A2 progression:** A1 handles yes/no. A2 repairs the plan rather than ending at the decline.

**Listening Preview:** recommended.

---

### A2-PL-03 — Ask about a leisure activity and decide whether to join

**Goal:** ask practical questions about a familiar class/event/activity, understand the basic details, and decide whether it suits you.

**Communication problem:** `ask information + preference + decision`

**Likely shape:** ask about activity -> ask time/price/what to bring or skill level -> hear answer -> state preference/decision -> confirm booking/attendance or close.

**A2 boundary:** the activity and requirements are straightforward. No complex membership contract or persuasive sales negotiation.

**Listening Preview:** optional/recommended.

## What A2 deliberately does not become

A2 missions should not require:

- a long explanation defending a position
- detailed narrative with complication/resolution
- complaint escalation or compensation negotiation
- multiple competing constraints
- sustained disagreement
- complex troubleshooting
- strategic persuasion
- nuanced register/tact management
- mediation between people

Those are intentionally reserved for B1–C2 progression.

# Cross-level progression checks from A1

Before approving A2, confirm that repeated contexts have a different communication problem:

```text
A1 order one item
-> A2 order with preferences / ask about options

A1 ask where a place is
-> A2 follow a short route and clarify one step

A1 buy one item
-> A2 compare two items / exchange a simple unsuitable item

A1 simple hotel check-in
-> A2 check in plus one secondary request

A1 book an appointment
-> A2 change/cancel an existing appointment

A1 state a visible service problem
-> A2 describe simple symptoms and arrange a next step

A1 make a simple plan
-> A2 express preference/reason and handle one alternative

A1 ask for repetition
-> A2 identify the unclear detail and ask targeted clarification
```

If a mission cannot explain its progression this clearly, it should be rewritten or removed.
